About Sam's Comics

Sam's Comics began when I was around six years old. What started as simple drawings gradually became a creative universe filled with original characters, stories, and ideas.

Over the years, my art and storytelling have developed. The Sam's Comics Universe, also known as SCMU, represents how far those early ideas have grown and how much more I still want to create.

The Beginning

I started drawing comics as a young kid, learning and improving along the way.

Creative Ideas

Characters, fictional worlds, and original stories are at the center of my comics.

What Comes Next

Although I've spent time focusing on coding and other projects, I hope to return to creating more comics in the future.
